# Local savvy — check before recommending

## Calendar 2026
- 20 Sep (Sun), 10 Oct (Sat): adjusted WORKING days
- 25–27 Sep: 中秋节 (Zhōngqiūjié) — 月饼 (yuèbǐng) mooncake gift needed before the 25th, brand 稻香村 (Dàoxiāngcūn), both hands
- 1–7 Oct: 国庆节 Golden Week — avoid Beijing-side entirely

## Scams — say the name, say skip
- 东华门夜市 (Dōnghuámén Yèshì) — closed since 2016, doesn't exist
- 王府井小吃街 (Wángfǔjǐng xiǎochījiē) — tourist theatre, skip for food
- Unprompted good-English approach near a monument ("student practicing English" → teahouse, or "art exhibit") = scam. Response: **Bù yòng, xièxie.** 不用，谢谢。 Keep walking.
- Unlicensed taxi 黑车 (hēichē) — use 滴滴 (Dīdī) instead

## Price benchmarks (¥, ×200≈₩)
- Taxi: ¥13 flagfall/3km, ¥2.30/km to 15km, **¥3.45/km beyond 15km**
- Beijing CBD → 燕郊: ¥130–170 clean, more with checkpoint wait
- Subway: ¥3–10 by distance band
- Bus: ¥2 (≤10km) + ¥1/5km after
- Luckin coffee: ¥13 w/ app coupon, ¥23–26 list
- Mid-range dinner/head: ~¥75
- 故宫 (Gùgōng) ticket: ¥60 peak (Apr–Oct) / ¥40 off-peak
- 景山公园 (Jǐngshān Gōngyuán): ¥10

Foreigner-price tell: quote is 3x the row above → walk.

## Timing
Lunch 11:30–13:00 (many kitchens close 14:00–17:00). Dinner 17:30–20:00 (earlier than Seoul). Weekends worse than weekdays at sights. Museums closed Mondays.

## Small things that read as local
说慢一点 (shuō màn yìdiǎn) instead of switching to English. Scan their code, not yours. Carry addresses in characters (pinyin is for his mouth, characters for their eyes).
