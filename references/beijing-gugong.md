# 故宫 (Gùgōng) — Forbidden City

**Entrance: 午门 (Wǔmén) ONLY, south side.** 东华门 (Dōnghuámén, east) and 神武门 (Shénwǔmén, north) are EXIT only.

Booking mandatory, no gate sales: WeChat mini-program 故宫博物院, 7 days ahead, passport must match booking exactly. Closed Mondays. ¥60 peak (Apr–Oct) / ¥40 off-peak.

Inside: one-way south→north, no backtrack. Food inside is poor value — eat outside.

## Outside food
- 四季民福 (Sìjì Mínfú) — Peking duck, 3min from 东华门, expect a queue
- 方砖厂69号 (Fāngzhuānchǎng 69 hào) — hand-pulled noodles, Michelin Bib Gourmand. Good 叔叔-conversation bait re: his noodles.
- Skip 东华门夜市 — closed since 2016, doesn't exist despite what blogs say

## Nearby
景山公园 (Jǐngshān Gōngyuán) — opposite north exit, ¥10, best view of the palace from above.
北海公园 (Běihǎi Gōngyuán) — ¥10, lake + pagoda.

## Getting home to Yanjiao
⚠ Beijing-side, ~35km, crosses 白庙检查站 checkpoint. Budget 90min+. 滴滴 (Dīdī) more reliable than street taxi for Hebei runs — many refuse.
> **Nín qù Yānjiāo ma?** 您去燕郊吗？
> **Shīfu, qù Héběi Yānjiāo, Yàndá Yīyuàn fùjìn, xièxie.** 师傅，去河北燕郊，燕达医院附近，谢谢。
