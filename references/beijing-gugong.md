# Reference — 故宫博物院 (Gùgōng Bówùyuàn), the Palace Museum

Korean: 고궁. English: the Forbidden City / Palace Museum.
Retrieve this file when asked about the Forbidden City or anything around it.

**All links are Apple Maps search links** — they resolve against live Amap data
rather than any coordinate I'd be recalling. Positions are therefore
authoritative; everything else on this page needs the confidence flags noted.

---

## 1. The gate rule — the thing that ruins visits

| Gate | Direction | Use |
|---|---|---|
| [午门 (Wǔmén) — Meridian Gate](https://maps.apple.com/?q=%E5%8D%88%E9%97%A8) | South | **ENTRY ONLY. The only way in.** |
| [神武门 (Shénwǔmén) — Gate of Divine Might](https://maps.apple.com/?q=%E7%A5%9E%E6%AD%A6%E9%97%A8) | North | Exit only. Puts you at Jingshan Park. |
| [东华门 (Dōnghuámén) — East Glorious Gate](https://maps.apple.com/?q=%E4%B8%9C%E5%8D%8E%E9%97%A8) | East | **Exit only.** Not an entrance. |
| 西华门 (Xīhuámén) — West Glorious Gate | West | Not open to the public |

**From 东华门 to 午门 is ~1.3 km on foot, 15–20 min**, around the moat south
then west. Anyone standing at Donghuamen expecting to walk in is about to lose
half an hour. Flag this every single time.

Movement inside is **one-way south→north**. You cannot backtrack. Decide what
you want to see before entering.

## 2. Booking — mandatory, no gate sales

| | |
|---|---|
| Where | WeChat mini-program 故宫博物院 (Gùgōng Bówùyuàn), or the official website |
| When released | **7 days ahead.** Weekends and holidays sell out fast. |
| Real-name | **Passport number must match the booking exactly.** Bring the physical passport. |
| Slots | Morning 08:30–12:00 / Afternoon 11:00–17:00 |
| **Closed** | **Mondays**, except some public holidays |
| Price | ~¥60 peak (Apr–Oct), ~¥40 low season (≈₩11,700 / ₩7,800 at ¥1≈₩195). 珍宝馆 (Zhēnbǎoguǎn) Treasure Gallery a small extra. **Verify — prices and gallery inclusion change.** |

⚠️ There is no walk-up option. No ticket = no entry, regardless of how far you travelled.

## 3. Inside — facilities

| What | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Visitor Service Centre | Left side immediately inside 午门 (Wǔmén) | Info, maps, assistance. First stop. |
| Washrooms 洗手间 (xǐshǒujiān) | 14 across the site | Reliable ones: near 午门 (Wǔmén), near 保和殿 (Bǎohédiàn) Hall of Preserving Harmony, and at the north exit. Western-style, accessible stalls. |
| Café | Near 保和殿 (Bǎohédiàn) | Snacks, drinks, ice cream. **Limited and overpriced** — consistent across sources. |
| [故宫冰窖餐厅 (Gùgōng Bīngjiào Cāntīng) — Ice Cellar Restaurant](https://maps.apple.com/?q=%E6%95%85%E5%AE%AB%E5%86%B0%E7%AA%96%E9%A4%90%E5%8E%85) | Inside, west side | Built into a genuine Qing-dynasty ice cellar. The one interesting meal inside. **Verify hours on 大众点评 (Dàzhòng Diǎnpíng).** |
| Water refill | Scattered fountains / café taps | Bring a bottle. Saves money and queueing. |

**Plan to eat outside.** Every source agrees food inside is poor value.

## 4. Outside — food

| Place | Type | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [四季民福 (Sìjì Mínfú)](https://maps.apple.com/?q=%E5%9B%9B%E5%AD%A3%E6%B0%91%E7%A6%8F%E7%83%A4%E9%B8%AD%E5%BA%97) | 烤鸭 (kǎoyā) Peking duck | ~3 min walk from 东华门 | Window seats look onto the corner tower and moat. Beijing-favourite for duck. **Expect a long queue — take a number early.** Verify on 大众点评. |
| [方砖厂69号 (Fāngzhuānchǎng 69 hào)](https://maps.apple.com/?q=%E6%96%B9%E7%A0%96%E5%8E%8269%E5%8F%B7%E7%82%B8%E9%85%B1%E9%9D%A2) | 炸酱面 (zhájiàngmiàn) noodles | Nearby hutong | **Michelin Bib Gourmand six years running, 2020–2026.** Hand-pulled wheat noodles, slow-cooked pork sauce. |
| "Mingyuan" — Beijing cuisine | 家常菜 (jiācháng cài) | Just east of 东华门 | 33 years, Michelin-recommended, open kitchen. **I could not confirm the Chinese characters** — search it in Apple Maps rather than trusting my spelling. |
| [王府井 (Wángfǔjǐng)](https://maps.apple.com/?q=%E7%8E%8B%E5%BA%9C%E4%BA%95) | Street food strip | ~10 min east | Stalls ~10:00–22:00. Touristy; fine for snacks, not a meal. |

⚠️ **东华门夜市 (Dōnghuámén Yèshì), the Donghuamen Night Market, closed permanently in 2016.** Old guidebooks and blogs still list it. It does not exist. Do not go looking.

### The noodle angle
方砖厂69号 is a **hand-pulled noodle** specialist with a Michelin listing. 叔叔
(shūshu) makes noodles. Eating there gives you a genuine, specific,
non-flattering thing to ask him about — how their 炸酱面 (zhájiàngmiàn) compares
to his. That is worth more than any gift conversation.

| Place | Why | Cost |

| [北海公园 (Běihǎi Gōngyuán) — Beihai Park](https://maps.apple.com/?q=%E5%8C%97%E6%B5%B7%E5%85%AC%E5%9B%AD) | Lake, white pagoda, boats. Northwest, 10 min walk. | ~¥10 (≈₩1,950) |

## 6. Phrases

| Situation | Say |
|---|---|
| Finding the entrance | **Qǐngwèn, Wǔmén zěnme zǒu?** 请问，午门怎么走？ [Excuse me, how do I get to the Meridian Gate?] |
| Washroom | **Xǐshǒujiān zài nǎr?** 洗手间在哪儿？ |
| At the gate | **Wǒ yùyuē le, zhè shì wǒ de hùzhào.** 我预约了，这是我的护照。 [I have a reservation, here's my passport.] |
| Queue length | **Yào děng duō jiǔ?** 要等多久？ [How long is the wait?] |

## 7. Getting home to 燕郊 (Yānjiāo)

⚠️ **Beijing-side.** The Forbidden City is ~35 km straight-line from home and
the return crosses 白庙检查站 (Báimiào Jiǎncházhàn). Budget **90 min+**, more at
rush hour. Many Beijing taxis refuse Hebei runs — 滴滴 (Dīdī) is more reliable.

> **Nín qù Yānjiāo ma?** 您去燕郊吗？ [Do you go to Yanjiao?]
>
> **Shīfu, qù Héběi Yānjiāo, Yàndá Yīyuàn fùjìn, xièxie.**
> 师傅，去河北燕郊，燕达医院附近，谢谢。

**Leave by 16:00** if you want to avoid the worst of the checkpoint.
