# Reference — 汉服妆造 (hànfú zhuāngzào): traditional dress, styling, photos

Researched 2026-09-12. What the girlfriend is booking when she rents
traditional clothing with hair and makeup near the Forbidden City.

## Vocabulary — search with these, not with English

| Term | Pinyin | Means |
|---|---|---|
| 汉服 | hànfú | Han-style traditional dress (Tang/Ming/Song styles) |
| 旗袍 | qípáo | Qipao / cheongsam — different thing, Qing-derived |
| 清宫装 | qīnggōngzhuāng | Qing palace costume — the Forbidden City look |
| **妆造** | **zhuāngzào** | **Makeup + hair styling.** The word that matters — rental without 妆造 is just clothes |
| 跟拍 | gēnpāi | Follow-along photographer |
| 旅拍 | lǚpāi | Travel photoshoot (the umbrella term) |
| 精修 | jīngxiū | Professionally retouched photos |
| 底片 | dǐpiàn | Raw/unedited shots |
| 押金 | yājīn | Refundable deposit |

Searching "hanfu rental Beijing" gets tourist-agency packages at a markup.
Searching **汉服妆造** or **故宫旅拍** on 大众点评 gets the actual market.

⏱️ **Allow 3–5 hours** from choosing clothes to finishing the shoot. Half to
full day if multiple locations. This is not a one-hour errand.

## ⚠️ The trap nobody mentions

**Commercial photography inside 故宫 (Gùgōng) is prohibited without the
museum's permission.** Wearing 汉服 and taking your own photos is ordinary
sightseeing and completely fine. **Hiring a photographer to shoot you inside
is not.**

Studios still advertise "故宫跟拍" packages. What they actually deliver is
shot *outside* the walls — the 角楼 (jiǎolóu) corner tower, the moat, 景山
(Jǐngshān). Those are genuinely the better photos anyway, but **confirm where
the shoot actually happens before paying**, or the package sells you an
impossibility.

## ⚠️ The review landscape is astroturfed

Searching for "best hanfu studio Beijing" surfaces long Chinese "selection
guides" and "avoid-the-pitfalls" articles that turn out to be **published by
the studios themselves** — the byline is a shop name. They exist to rank in
search and recommend their author.

**This is why I will not name a best studio.** Any ranking I gave would be
laundering an advertorial. 大众点评 (Dàzhòng Diǎnpíng) has no public API and
blocks scraping, so I have no independent review data either.

**The girlfriend picks this one.** She reads 点评 natively and can smell a
fake review; I can't do either. My job is the checklist below.

## The checklist — send her this

Before paying, get these confirmed **in writing in the chat**:

| Chinese | Pinyin | What you're pinning down |
|---|---|---|
| 妆造包含发型和饰品吗？ | zhuāngzào bāohán fàxíng hé shìpǐn ma? | Does styling include hair AND accessories? |
| 一共多少张底片？精修几张？ | yígòng duōshao zhāng dǐpiàn? jīngxiū jǐ zhāng? | How many raw shots, how many retouched? |
| 加修一张多少钱？ | jiāxiū yì zhāng duōshao qián? | Cost per extra retouched photo |
| 超时怎么收费？ | chāoshí zěnme shōufèi? | Overtime charges |
| 押金多少？怎么退？ | yājīn duōshao? zěnme tuì? | Deposit amount and refund process |
| 外景门票谁出？ | wàijǐng ménpiào shéi chū? | Who pays location entry tickets |
| 道具另外收费吗？ | dàojù lìngwài shōufèi ma? | Are props charged separately |
| 在哪里拍？故宫里面可以吗？ | zài nǎlǐ pāi? Gùgōng lǐmiàn kěyǐ ma? | **Where exactly — and is inside the palace actually allowed?** |

The documented pattern is **低价引流 (dījià yǐnliú)** — a cheap headline price
that pulls you in, then accessories, props, retouching and overtime are billed
on top. A quote that doesn't answer the eight questions above is not a quote.

## ⏰ Timing — book now

中秋节 (Zhōngqiūjié) 25–27 Sep and 国庆节 (Guóqìngjié) 1–7 Oct. These studios
book out and raise prices across both. If this is happening in September,
**reserve before the 20th** or accept holiday pricing and no slots.
