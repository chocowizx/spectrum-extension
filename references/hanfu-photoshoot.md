# 汉服妆造 (hànfú zhuāngzào) — traditional dress + makeup/photo

**Cluster: 王府世纪大厦 (Wángfǔ Shìjì Dàshà), 东安门大街55号.** 500+ studios in one building, southeast of the palace. Go to upper floors (quieter, less rushed than ground-floor walk-in traffic). Compare 3–4 shops in person, negotiate — high supply density.

No reliable review source exists (大众点评 blocked from scraping, Chinese "guide" articles are studio self-promotion) — don't trust a "best studio" ranking, including mine. Let her pick on the spot.

Price: ¥200–400 basic (costume+makeup, no photographer). ¥600–1,200 standard (+1-2hr shoot). ~¥800 refundable deposit (押金 yājīn).

**Commercial photography INSIDE 故宫 is not permitted** — advertised "故宫跟拍" shoots actually happen outside the walls (角楼 jiǎolóu corner tower, moat). Confirm location before paying.

## Questions to confirm before paying
- 妆造包含发型和饰品吗？(Zhuāngzào bāohán fàxíng hé shìpǐn ma?) — styling incl. hair+accessories?
- 一共多少张底片？精修几张？(Yígòng duōshao zhāng dǐpiàn? Jīngxiū jǐ zhāng?) — raw/retouched count
- 加修一张多少钱？(Jiāxiū yì zhāng duōshao qián?) — extra retouch cost
- 超时怎么收费？(Chāoshí zěnme shōufèi?) — overtime
- 押金多少？怎么退？(Yājīn duōshao? Zěnme tuì?) — deposit/refund
- 道具另外收费吗？(Dàojù lìngwài shōufèi ma?) — props extra?
- 在哪里拍？故宫里面可以吗？(Zài nǎlǐ pāi? Gùgōng lǐmiàn kěyǐ ma?) — where exactly, palace interior allowed?

Book before 20 Sep — 中秋/国庆 holidays fill studios and raise prices.
